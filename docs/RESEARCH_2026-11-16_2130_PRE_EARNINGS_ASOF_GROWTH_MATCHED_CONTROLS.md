# November 16, 2026 — 21:30 CT research-only pre-earnings controls

**Status: exploratory negative/indeterminate evidence; no verified after-fee positive forward edge.** No deployment, broker orders, changes to admission limits, service/timers, or alerts.

### Method

Read the frozen `/var/lib/securityprepper-earnings-anticipation/` snapshots: `latest.json`, the 3,809 ordinary-stock earnings-calendar symbol/date pairs, and the 98 priority-sector last-earnings events. The Python script `research/v2_20261116_2130_preearnings_matched_diagnostic.py` implements deterministic symbol/date joins, checks uniqueness, then chooses up to 20 non-priority sector ordinary-stock control events **on the identical Nasdaq-listed earnings date** with the nearest historical 20-day median dollar volume. It does not use later prices for control selection, and does not use today's market-cap estimate for matching. The matched results use 4,000 deterministic resamples of 11 earnings-week clusters to give an uncertainty indication, not a prospective efficacy claim.

### Historical observations, not live trade outcomes

- **98/98** priority sector events matched, with a 5-session pre-release CLOSE increase in **26/98 (26.53%)**. The 20-liquidity-nearest, same-date nonsector control events showed a mean **58.24%** of positive close returns. Sector mean pre-earnings 5-day return **−3.42%**, matched-control mean **+0.42%**, event-weighted average sector minus control **−3.84 percentage points**; week-cluster bootstrap 95% bounds **−6.30 to −0.90 percentage points**. This is descriptive only, not a reason to short the sector, cancel protections, or change the existing strategy: the sector universe was selected retrospectively, calendars are not issuer-confirmed, control pools are reused, and there are only 11 effective calendar-week clusters.
- Among **171** current upcoming candidates, **86** had sufficiently comparable past annual SEC growth percentage values; **12** met the *latest-year* 15% revenue and EPS thresholds and **5** met both of the latest two years. Only the same 12 and 5 symbols respectively had historical pre-earnings events in the broad file with SEC reported `last_filed` dates preceding the historical five-day entry. In the 12 latest-year events, **4/12 (33.3%)** had positive 5-session close returns (Wilson 95% interval **13.8–60.9%**); under the stricter two-year rule, **1/5 (20%)** (Wilson 95% interval **3.6–62.5%**). These all clustered in August–September (five strict cases all August), and newer-retrieved SEC history can be restated. Consequently neither rule has demonstrated a positive expectancy, and loosening it based on these sparse retrospectively matched data would be unjustified.

### Causality, fill and trading gates

All observed returns use split-adjusted historical SIP daily **close** values, not verified ASK entry, BID noon-before-earnings exit, commissions/slippage, stop/gap fills, or broker-specific protection. Earnings dates lack independent original issuer timing. No immutable historical same-quarter EPS consensus snapshots or original `first_seen` timestamps exist, so results cannot condition on **rising EPS revisions**, a key part of the requested hypothesis. Historical SEC annual values satisfy only a necessary `filed_at <= prior6_day` condition, not proof the retrieved XBRL historical version matches what was available then. `NEWS_CLEAR_ASOF`, position/order/buying-power and multi-day stops remain unverified; all such events are research-only. There are no robust, independent 20–30 forward-session results or real after-fee PAPER fills here, hence **NO GO**.
