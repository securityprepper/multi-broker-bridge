# November 10, 2026 — MBB session idempotency and stale-position provenance stress (V2 research ONLY)

**New safety-relevant source findings; not evidence of actual mis-execution.** Reproduced offline using the currently installed November 10 MBB functions, only in-memory SQLite, and a fake broker stub. No Alpaca/IBKR order calls, active-code edits, service restarts or account changes were made.

## 1. The same overnight trading session is split into two order reservation dates

`auto_paper.run` uses `now.astimezone(America/New_York).date()` for `attempts.trade_date`, the `_reserve` primary key and the MBB client-order ID. The strategy separately defines `trading_session_day(now)` in America/Chicago, mapping 19:00 CT onward into **the next** trading session. At **November 9 21:30 CST**, the session is November 10 but the reservation date is November 9. At **November 9 23:30 CST**, it is **the same November 10 session**, but the reservation date is now November 10. A source-code-based, in-memory test accepted **two** same-symbol/same-side reservations in this one trading session. Reserving both under `trading_session_day` correctly rejected the second.

**Scope:** This demonstrates a *latent idempotency-key inconsistency*, not any double-filled real order. The installed `tracked` table provides an additional gate in many situations; an explicitly broker-verified zero-fill terminal cleanup or a repaired tracking workflow could expose the duplicate reservation risk if date normalization is omitted. Read-only current SQLite showed 34 November 9 BUY attempts, one FLATTEN and 30 SELL attempts, **no November 10 attempts** during this check. Therefore no actual split-session duplicate was observed.

## 2. A stale MBB tracked row can misattribute later same-symbol shares to MBB

The mandatory 13:40 CT `paper_time_exit.timed_flatten` reads all persistent tracked symbols without a session/provenance check. Alpaca's aggregate account position for a symbol does not say which strategy bought the shares. When a *hypothetical* previous-day MBB tracked row for AMD specifies 4 shares but the broker aggregate now shows 2 AMD shares belonging exclusively to a different strategy, and there is no open unmanaged order, the installed function sent **one MARKET SELL for 2 shares to an in-memory fake broker**. It checked only the symbol and that 2 <= previous tracked quantity. This could liquidate unrelated shares if this ownership ambiguity arose in real PAPER operation. **No such actual other-strategy position or mistaken sell was observed or claimed.**

The existing safeguards DO reject an unmanaged open order and a broker position larger than the tracked quantity. A flat broker snapshot causes no sale, but also leaves the tracked row retained; so the stale symbol can remain eligible for future misattribution. The code's current guarantee that the mandatory flatten never touches manually owned shares is therefore not established for a same-symbol replacement position of equal/smaller quantity.

## 3. Combined safe remediation requirements — proposal only

Do not delete tracked rows, disable flatten, change PAPER orders or activate V2. First, introduce a normalized session key computed from `trading_session_day` for **all** order reservations, client IDs and reentry lookup; migrate previous keys without permitting duplicates or accidentally recycling prior references. Second, separate protective *historical MBB obligation* from current-session *entry permission*, and verify prior broker filled entry/sell order IDs, activity UUIDs, position/open-order inventory, any unknown/partial orders and attribution across MBB/FDR/manual writers. Third, do not infer strategy ownership from aggregate symbol plus a quantity ceiling. Ambiguous or externally owned shares require a fail-closed ownership exception with explicit reconciliation, preserving valid MBB exit protection. Test the 19:00 CT rollover, 23:00 CT / 00:00 ET boundary, DST, canceled-zero-fill, partial fills, an overlapping other-strategy position and the unchanged 13:15/13:40/14:00, holiday, PAPER/LIVE gates. Review separately before any authorized PAPER deployment.

The confirmed November 10 00:20 BUY_WATCH blocks arose from yesterday's retained tracking; they are not proof of executable or profitable missed fills.
