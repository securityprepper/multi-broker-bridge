## Track 2 — does five-minute continuation rescue semiconductor urgent SELL precision?

The original owner-defined alert is **at least -2.5% TOTAL** from prior regular close **INCLUDING** at least -1.0 percentage point over the most recent completed 15 minutes, with SOXX and peer breadth confirmation. The two thresholds overlap and are **not** additive. This new replay observes the original alert first, then waits an additional 5 or 10 minutes, demands stock-only or stock+SOXX additional declines of 0/0.10/0.25 percentage points since that alert, and models the sale at a further **5-minute delayed** bar OPEN. Outcomes are strictly later and never used to qualify a SELL. This is an optional confidence layer over immediate WATCH, not a replacement for urgent risk alerts or existing protective exits.

**Cohort:** 2,235 comparable split-adjusted SIP stock-days, **209 severe <=-5% close-to-prior-close declines** (160 earlier, 49 later). Existing 250-sector-session status baseline = **118 positive / 83 negative / 49 mixed**; an independently broader original positive outcome count is 254 major up stock-days. Original owner-overlap-plus-breadth alert: earlier **149/160** major hits, **304** nonmajor alerts; later **39/49**, **58** nonmajor alerts. Hypothetical earlier-than-next-regular-opening SELL advantage at 30bp was **-0.9659pp earlier and -0.0321pp later**, with only ~40% beneficial early exits in either window.

The 5-minute **stock+SOXX both weakening >=0.10pp after the original alert** had just **60/209** full-cohort major hits and **98** nonmajor warnings. In the later period it caught **11/49** severe declines, emitted **9** nonmajor warnings, and showed **11/20 = 55.0%** hypothetical beneficial exits versus holding to the following regular opening, with only **+0.0713pp** mean advantage. In the earlier period it issued **138** alerts, only **42.03%** would have benefited from early sale, and modeled advantage averaged **-1.1017pp**. This is a **regime reversal**, not dependable 80%-precision exit logic. Earliest full-year major warning under this extra-confirmed version was **Nov 13, 2025, 09:45 ET**, versus **08:35 ET** for the underlying original strict rule. Critically Nov 2, 2026 the confirmed example was **WDC at 15:40 ET**, not preopen protection, while other severe semiconductor names remained missed. The 10-minute/0.25pp alternatives also lost serious-loss coverage.

**Conclusion:** Do not replace immediate WATCH with delayed confirmation, and do not automatically sell from this candidate. Raising stringency suppresses false positives but misses important crashes, including overnight gaps; mean modeled value is unstable and not executable. Need as-of one-minute/futures/news observations before the dangerous move, true SIP/BOATS NBBO and order-route constraints, plus matched V1 PAPER fills and proper independent walk-forward performance.
