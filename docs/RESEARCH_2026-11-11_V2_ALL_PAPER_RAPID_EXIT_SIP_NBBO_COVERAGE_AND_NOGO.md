Each position's hypothetical analysis uses only bars beginning strictly *after* its real broker BUY final-fill time. A complete 1-minute path through the 13:40 CT evaluation existed for only **40/60** trades. **16/60 had at least one missing internal minute** and **4/60** lacked a sufficiently prompt first post-entry minute. No missing bar was filled with a lookahead future price, and missing data were excluded from exact stop/target-path comparison.

Independent historical SIP bid/ask quote queries were made for **each of the 120 actual entry/exit final-fill timestamps**, using only quotes at or before the fill: **62/120** had an admissible quote at most 1.5 seconds old with noncrossed spread at most 1% and displayed best-side size at least that order's quantity; **33** were stale and **25** unavailable. The 62 included 33 BUY and 29 SELL observations; an observed quote does not prove the order could have filled at that price or establish actual commissions.

Separately, a snapshot immediately **before 13:40 CT** retrieved historical SIP bid/ask for 52 distinct symbol/day pairs: **44 fresh within two seconds**, **five stale**, **three unavailable**. In the event a completed minute could have crossed both target and stop, the conservative order is **STOP FIRST**. The hypothetical decision is made only after the triggering minute completes and the *next* minute's trade **OPEN** is used as a **non-executable price mark**, never the intrabar high, stop or target as a guaranteed fill.
