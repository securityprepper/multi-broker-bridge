## Interpretation / non-go

No statistically robust positive forward edge is established. The apparent average is consistent with meaningful losses and extremely sensitive to 11 report-date blocks and outlier tails. The trial is historical, not 20–30 untouched forward sessions; even the November date separation does not repair historical event-date / EPS first-seen hindsight or substitute for real quote/fill data. **Do not commission PRE_EARNINGS_ANTICIPATION_V1 orders or change risk/entry thresholds based on this.** Continue collecting verified pre-previous-release EPS consensus vintages, previous-report actual EPS with matched basis, original upcoming EPS at entry, issuer release evidence, yesterday/today adverse-news clearance, original bid/ask, previous-day noon exit and forward PAPER-only reconciliation when separately authorized.
