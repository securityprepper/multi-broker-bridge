## New independent profit hypothesis: post-earnings guidance revision + controlled first-pullback
This is NOT another price-only sector continuation variant. Research a bounded, timestamped *fundamental information shock* in liquid quality stocks:
- Anchor the event to official IR/company earnings press-release time and/or SEC 8-K acceptance time. Collect original guidance and revisions to revenue, EPS/free cash flow where disclosed, plus prior consensus with a **known-before-event timestamp**; a simple positive EPS surprise alone is insufficient. Distinguish an actual guidance upgrade from optimism based on price rise.
- Require independent contemporaneous material news confirmation (not reposts), no pending market-wide macro event, and actual market/limit eligibility. Record first news receipt, first objectively tradable quote, bid/ask depth/spread and first 1/5/15-minute price reaction.
- Candidate: after an upside guidance surprise, require an initial significant but non-exhausted response, a subsequent *first* orderly pullback/volume contraction and renewed bid-supported breakout; use fixed causal 1m/5m VWAP and high-water prices only from completed bars. A negative guide-down counterpart is risk WATCH / potential short simulation only, never a broker order by this research.
- Enumerate ALL earnings events across at least 52 weeks, not hindsight winners. Compare initial post-release impulse, first pullback, delayed 15m/30m entry and no-trade, including late/missing events, illiquid spread, headline corrections, halts and false positives. Train on earlier events only; seal policy before 20+ genuinely new prospective event days. Prefer predeclared quality/liquidity minimums and broad industry mix.
- For each event compute post-signal forward 15m/60m/13:40 Chicago outcomes, bid/ask simulated fills and after-fee results, loss tail, abstention, number of trades and false signals. A first-pullback candidate requires **positive, statistically credible after-cost forward expectancy**, not 80% retrospectively labeled daily direction. If it fails a prospective loss/precision gate, retire it rather than tune on the same data.
- Optional second independent candidate only after this: independently timestamped analyst *estimate revision* clusters with source-time confirmation and pre/post open event windows; do not reuse price-only sector templates.
