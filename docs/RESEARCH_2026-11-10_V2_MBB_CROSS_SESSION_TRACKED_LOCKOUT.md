**Root cause in installed code:** auto_paper.py reads the full persistent tracked table without session filtering (approximately lines 329–332). Any subsequent BUY_WATCH whose symbol exists in tracked is forced through the exceptional profitable discounted-reentry predicate (approximately lines 513–519). The only visible tracked-row delete path applies to a terminal canceled/expired/rejected entry with zero fills, **not** to a filled exit or next trading session. The reentry predicate correctly limits profitable exits to the same trading_session_day (19:00 CST maps to the following session). Consequently, a November 9 completed trade retained in tracked can prevent the **first permitted November 10 entry**. This is not the same as verifying the **November 9 03:18 AMD skip**, which remains undetermined because its old journal omitted buy_watch_symbols and skipped_buy_reasons.

**Remediation recommendation, not deployed:** separate protective broker-open tracking from per-session first-entry/reentry eligibility. On a new trading_session_day, only rearm a symbol after verified broker flatness, no open or UNKNOWN/partial orders, reconciliation of prior fills/exits, session-specific idempotent reservation and all normal spread/cash/sector gates. Do **not** indiscriminately clear tracked rows: doing so could lose mandatory exits on still-open or delayed positions. Preserve exceptional two-percent verified profitable sale plus discounted-ask eligibility for actual same-session reentries, the 13:15 CST cutoff, the 13:40 mandatory flatten, the 14:00 verification, holiday gate and all LIVE prohibitions.
