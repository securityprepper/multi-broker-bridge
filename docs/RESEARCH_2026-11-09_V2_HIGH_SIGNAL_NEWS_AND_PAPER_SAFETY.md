## Four desired alert types and distinct outcomes
- **Unusually strong sector UP/DOWN:** report a verified factual extreme with timestamp and holdings/exposure if important; candidate auto-close/entry remains NOT commissioned pending after-signal continuation/false-positive validation. Overnight action requires a verified market session and 24/5 software stop; the native bracket will not protect outside regular hours.
- **Approaching earnings, new earnings release:** news feed can catch some scheduled-earnings mentions and published articles, but does not provide a complete earnings calendar; must verify official release plus guidance and price reaction; reject stale headline/market gap and stop the trade if relevant macro/catalyst negative.
- **Other material positive company news:** one article/headline alone is insufficient; independently verify event source, timestamp and materially positive implication; ensure price remains positive but not extended, liquid, and offers positive forward expected edge.
- **Four Trading Opportunities tasks:** scan different market areas, claim work in shared Percona before research, avoid duplicate orders, report only BUY NOW or PULLBACK LIMIT with limit/profit/stop/whole shares and verified broker PAPER execution status. Hourly ChatGPT tasks do not replace host minute streaming execution; safe shared PAPER executor must be separately commissioned.
