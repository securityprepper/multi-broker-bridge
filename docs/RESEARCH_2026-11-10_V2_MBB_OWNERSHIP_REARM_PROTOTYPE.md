## Safety model and separations

The new `research/v2_mbb_session_ownership_gate.py` is a research-only classification function, with no broker, SQL, API, file or network calls. It tests the two distinct errors documented in earlier V2 audits:

1. **New-session first purchase**: prior-day tracked history must not automatically count as today's reentry. The November 9 19:00 CST transition belongs to the November 10 trading session both before and after the New York midnight boundary. A new-session first entry is *only eligible for normal checks* after complete, fresh broker positions/orders, activity UUID reconciliation, a verified prior-flat anchor, absence of open/unknown orders, and absence of current-session attempts. It does **not** submit a buy, bypass quote or sector checks, or permit repeated same-day losses to be re-entered.
2. **Mandatory exit ownership**: a lingering MBB tracked symbol alone never establishes ownership of the current account-level shares. A sale obligation can only be classified as verified when complete strategy-attributed fills reconcile to the aggregate broker position, no other writer has net shares or pending orders, and independent evidence proves an exclusive MBB symbol lease over the **whole holding interval**. Even an apparently matching symbol/quantity must fail closed without lease proof. An ambiguous position generates an explicit **BLOCK_AND_ESCALATE_OWNERSHIP** outcome: the live 13:40 mandatory exit still needs a separately reviewed reconciliation/escalation mechanism. This prototype does **not** justify leaving known MBB holdings open past the 2 p.m. latest-flat requirement.

Flat broker account inventory and 31 old tracked records are insufficient by themselves to certify every fill, UNKNOWN reservation, or shared-symbol ownership. Conversely, blindly deleting old rows can lose valid exit protection or misattribute subsequent shares. The reconciliation requires broker order IDs, fill UUIDs, all other strategy writers, outstanding/canceled/partial orders, and an independently proven exclusive ownership lock or isolation. The latter proof is **not provided by current source code or aggregate broker positions**; the prototype cannot infer it.
