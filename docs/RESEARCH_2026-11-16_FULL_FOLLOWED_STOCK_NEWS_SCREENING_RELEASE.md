# Full followed-stock news-screening coverage — 2026-11-16

## Production deployment and authorization
Owner requested expansion from the previous 25-issuer RSS catalog to **every stock normally followed**. The authorized www3 host’s three existing watch universes were reconciled: quality watcher 208, priority industry universe 98, Multi-Broker Bridge four sectors 54, yielding 259 distinct symbols before retired-symbol normalization. CYBR was replaced with its source-defined active successor PANW, leaving 258 active core names. Twenty-eight historically followed or owned names were added for **286 individual equities**. Industry ETFs (SOXX, GDX, XLK, etc.) are sector references, not individual issuers or corporate-news BUY clearances. The resulting root-owned universe reference lives at `/etc/securityprepper/recommendation-paper-bridge/normal-followed-universe.json`; two symbols (EA and OSP) do not currently match the locally available SEC CIK directory and are necessarily marked unknown. This is a dated, source-provenance snapshot; genuinely new MBB/quality candidates are separately admitted to diagnostics when independently SEC-mapped.

A separate GET-only service `securityprepper-news-universe-screener.service` and enabled `securityprepper-news-universe-screener.timer` were commissioned. Schedule each minute at :20 America/Chicago, after unchanged original :15 issuer collector and before unchanged root :45 clearance publisher. The new service reads the exact current authorized root-owned holiday gate, validates local timezone/revision/coverage/freshness, and fails closed if invalid or paused. It reads only the previous completed MBB service journal / quality-watch SQLite for on-demand symbol selection, prioritizes them, then rotates through all registered companies in batches up to 40 to control SEC/news-provider rate demand. It does not access brokerage orders/accounts nor place any trade. The root publisher continues to decide eligibility independently.

## Strict evidence distinction
- **25 previously authenticated issuer-owned HTTPS RSS sources** (24 belonging to the fixed 286-company universe) remain the only official issuer feeds known to the original collector. For these, the new service can write the exact existing THREE-SOURCE SEC+issuer+licensed-news manifest; root publisher independently rechecks original publication on Chicago YESTERDAY/TODAY calendar dates, SEC/ticker/exchange issuer identity, source first observed, receipts/freshness, materiality, and missing/contradictory evidence. Even having a valid feed never automatically gives BUY eligibility.
- For remaining companies with missing or forbidden issuer feeds, the new service still fetches **trusted SEC submissions and entitled Alpaca/Benzinga/fiscal.ai news** and records their independent first-observed receipts/events as a partial review. These diagnostics are **never inserted as a complete source bundle** and can never result in NEWS_CLEAR_ASOF. Ambiguous events are REVIEW/WATCH; no issuer, stale news, incomplete SEC identity, no publisher proof, or any failed source means NEWS_UNKNOWN/NO BUY. It is incorrect to say all 286 issuers are news-cleared.
- The private status ledger at `/var/lib/securityprepper-news-universe-screener/coverage.json` records the exact screening state, last-check time and independent source success/failure for each of 286 symbols. Untested symbols are NOT_YET_SCREENED with buy_clearance_eligible false. There are no synthetic/fabricated clean clearances.
- All existing MBB PAPER receiving-account limits/quotes/stops/13:15 entries/13:40 flatten, existing LIVE orders, Pushover, holiday publisher, existing collector and publisher timers are untouched.
