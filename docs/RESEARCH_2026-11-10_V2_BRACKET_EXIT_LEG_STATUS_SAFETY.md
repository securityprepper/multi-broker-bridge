# November 10, 2026 — unprotected canceled bracket leg must not be reported protected (research-only)

**New pre-release safety finding and tested correction.** The standalone recommendation-to-Alpaca PAPER bridge is **not commissioned**: router/reconcile/exit timers remain disabled; V1 PAPER/LIVE orders, broker accounts and production host checkouts were not changed. The flaw is in the research-branch bridge code only; it is **not** evidence that an existing V1 position lost its protection.

## Reproduced failure before fix

`recommendation_paper_bridge.reconcile.assess()` labeled a fully filled two-share BUY as `OPEN_WITH_BRACKET` whenever one target and one stop **object** existed under `parent.legs`, even if the stop's broker `status` was `canceled`, the target was `rejected`/`pending_cancel`, or the status was absent. Synthetic evidence: parent fill 2 shares, profit target `new`, stop `canceled`, zero exit FILL activities. Before the correction, `assess()` returned `OPEN_WITH_BRACKET` and `exit_guard.plan()` returned `NONE` ahead of the mandatory 13:40 Chicago close, even though a broker-canceled stop cannot provide protective exit. This could leave a later commissioned PAPER entry unprotected until the mandatory liquidation.

## Corrected research behavior

A *still-open* long is `OPEN_WITH_BRACKET` only if the parent has fully filled AND the verified take-profit leg is `new`/`accepted` AND the stop leg is `new`/`accepted`/`held`. **Alpaca's broker-native bracket normally has take-profit `new`, stop `held` after the parent fills**, so the status rules deliberately allow the held STOP, not a held TARGET (see [Alpaca bracket documentation](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/orders-at-alpaca) and [broker explanation of held stop leg](https://forum.alpaca.markets/t/half-of-bracket-order-held/2727)). A terminal/uncertain/missing child status makes the remaining fill `PARTIAL_UNPROTECTED`, even if the parent itself is completely filled. The existing exclusive-share ownership, foreign-order rejection, cancellation/readback and idempotent emergency-flatten checks remain mandatory; it is not permission to trade or to sell another writer's position. No fallback to unprotected synthetic overnight stops.

In isolated pre-fix tests the canceled-stop case returned `OPEN_WITH_BRACKET` and the 13:40 guard returned `NONE` before cutoff. After this code-only fix, that case returns `PARTIAL_UNPROTECTED` and the existing guard plans `FLATTEN_EXCLUSIVE_PAPER` before the 13:40 deadline **only after** its account ownership and order inventory checks. The normal held-stop `new` target remains `OPEN_WITH_BRACKET`; a held target, canceled stop, rejected/pending-cancel/missing target, and a missing leg are never classified as protected. Test tests/test_recommendation_paper_reconcile.py and tests/test_recommendation_paper_exit_guard.py.

**58 isolated synthetic recommendation PAPER tests passed** (53 preexisting + five new individual tests). No actual Alpaca broker calls, Percona mutations, order POSTs, timer enabling, V1 source edits, credential access, holiday changes or LIVE permission occurred.
