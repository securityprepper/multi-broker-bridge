## Confirmed cross-session lockout

At the bounded November 12 morning observation, **629** completed MBB scan records contained **75** BUY_WATCH appearances for **24** distinct symbols. Of those, **67** appearances across **21 distinct symbols** were rejected as `PRIOR_ENTRY_NOT_ELIGIBLE_FOR_DISCOUNTED_REENTRY`, linked to *previous-session* tracked entries: 58 appearances referenced November 9 entries, 7 November 10, and 2 November 11. Another 7 appearances were blocked as already extended beyond the 2% entry cap. Exactly one BUY order was recorded as `pending_new`; that is an acceptance, not fill evidence. Separate authenticated GET-only broker inspection subsequently confirmed **40 PAPER shares of VRNS filled**, with an open MBB fill quantity of 40 at that snapshot; no sell/closed round trip was established by it.

The private strategy database contained **52 tracked symbols**, including **51 carried over from November 9–11** (31 + 13 + 7). The previously verified broker-fill audit had independently found all 51 historical MBB round trips closed, with no remaining open MBB fill quantities before the new VRNS fill. The journal's prior-entry skip occurs only after the executor checks existing broker positions, pending orders, fresh quotes, holiday/session and extension conditions; it therefore identifies a real software admission blocker for those candidate appearances, **not** proof that these would have been safe or profitable buys.

Active source mechanism: `tracked` uses `symbol` as its primary key with **no trading-day restriction**; `run()` reads all tracked symbols every session. Once a BUY_WATCH reaches `if symbol in tracked:`, a new ordinary purchase is denied unless the exceptional verified cheaper-reentry test succeeds. That exception requires a fully filled same-session premarket sale, >=2% realized gain and >=0.25% cheaper ask, before 10:00 CT, with independent rising signal and broker safeguards. Tracking is deleted only after a verified **zero-fill** canceled/rejected/expired entry, not after a completed sale. In contrast, the separate `attempts` idempotency table is trade-date scoped. Thus a filled stock bought on an earlier session can stay in the new-session exclusion set indefinitely. This is inconsistent with the intended once-per-stock-*day* admission rule, and compromises prospective 20+ session evaluation. Fix requires a separately approved source change after fresh receiving-broker readbacks; simply deleting state would be unsafe.
